Chapter 02 · Lead Flow
Steps 1–4:
Capture to Route
1
Consumer fills out the form
Google or Meta ad brings them to our landing page. Name, phone, email, zip, what they need. Consent checkbox. IP + timestamp recorded automatically.
2
Intake Agent validates the lead
Real phone? Duplicate in last 30 days? Bot timing detected (under 3 seconds)? Passes → saved to database. Fails → rejected and logged. Client never sees junk.
3
Qualify Agent scores the lead 1–10
Claude AI reads the consumer's message and scores intent. "Roof leaking 2 weeks, need fix before winter" → 9. "yes" → 2. Score travels with the lead.
4
Route Agent matches lead to client
Checks: which client serves this niche + zip, has capacity, is active? Assigns the lead. Exclusive → one client only. Shared → up to 4 clients simultaneously.

Chapter 02 · Lead Flow
Steps 5–7:
Deliver to Invoice
5
Delivery Agent pushes to client CRM + alerts
Lead data sent to client's CRM (GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Salesforce, etc.) via webhook. Client gets SMS: "New lead — [Name], [Phone]. Score 8/10. Call now." Plus full email. Under 60 seconds total.
6
Follow-Up Agent monitors client response
No response in 4 hours → reminder SMS. 24 hours → second reminder. 48 hours → lead re-routed to backup client. Original client flagged as unresponsive.
7
Reports + Billing run on autopilot
Every Monday 8AM: each client gets their weekly report automatically. On the 1st of each month: Stripe invoices generated and sent. Zero manual work from us.
✅

Key point: A lead at 3AM Sunday gets validated, scored, routed, delivered, and the client is alerted — all before 3:01AM. We build it once. It runs forever.

🔴

Management Agent texts us with exactly what decision is needed. Loop pauses until we respond.

Chapter 04 · Risks Solved
Critical Risks —
Business-Ending
🤖
Lead Farm Fraud — Fake Human Submissions
CRITICAL
Organized operations submit fake but real-looking data. Industry average: 36% of all leads are fraudulent.
Our Fix
Carrier lookup, email domain age check, form timing analysis — Intake Agent rejects these before they hit our database.
⚖️
TCPA Violations — Calling Without Consent
CRITICAL
The #1 legal risk in the industry. One class action = $500,000+ average settlement.
Our Fix
Non-pre-checked consent checkbox on every form. Consent text, timestamp, and IP recorded per lead — our legal proof.
♻️
Aged Lead Reselling — Selling Stale as Fresh
CRITICAL
Hold leads 30–90 days, resell as new. Consumer solved their problem months ago. Destroys conversion rates.
Our Fix
Every lead includes its exact creation timestamp. Only leads under 24hrs old are delivered. Queue expires at 7 days.

Chapter 04 · Risks Solved
High + Legal Risks
⏱️
Lead Latency — Slow Delivery Kills Conversions
HIGH
Competitors deliver daily CSVs. Conversion drops 80% after 5 minutes. Clients blame lead quality — it's a delivery problem.
Our Fix
Delivery Agent fires within seconds. Lead in client CRM + SMS in under 60 seconds. This makes our leads 3–5× more valuable.
🔁
Exclusive Lead Double-Selling
HIGH
Client pays premium for exclusive, gets the same lead sent to a competitor. Trust destroyed instantly when clients compare notes.
Our Fix
Atomic database write in Route Agent — technically impossible to assign same exclusive lead twice. Red Team audits nightly.
🔒
GDPR / CCPA — No Data Deletion Policy
LEGAL
Any consumer in California or the EU can demand deletion. Fines up to $750/person (CCPA) or 4% revenue (GDPR).
Our Fix
Leads auto-archive at 90 days. Deletion request triggers automated removal + logged. Privacy policy on every form.
