Chapter 01
What We're Building
onkooz is adding a lead generation and distribution business. We generate leads from consumers looking for local services, then sell those leads to the businesses that serve them — with full automation, real-time delivery, and reporting on both sides.
The Business Model in Plain English
👤

Consumer side: Someone in Miami needs a new roof. They find our landing page, fill out a short form — name, phone, email, zip, what they need. They hit submit.

🏢

Business side: A roofing company in Miami has paid us to receive exactly these leads. Within 60 seconds of the form submit, they get the consumer's contact info pushed directly into their CRM, plus an SMS and email alert.

💰

How we make money: We charge the roofing company per lead — typically $30–$150 depending on the niche and whether it's exclusive. We generate the leads through paid ads (Google, Meta) and organic traffic. The margin is the difference between what we pay to acquire the lead and what we charge to deliver it.

Three Types of Leads We Sell
🎯
Exclusive Leads
Sold to one client only. Highest price ($75–$150). Consumer only gets called by one business. Higher close rate for the client. We only generate one lead capture form per niche per territory for exclusive deals.
🔀
Shared Leads
Sold to 2–4 clients simultaneously. Lower price ($25–$50). Consumer may get called by multiple businesses. Lower margin per lead but higher volume makes up for it.
🏭
Niche Verticals
We pick specific industries to dominate: Roofing, HVAC, Legal, Dental, Home Services. Starting with one niche and one territory makes everything simpler and lets us prove the model before scaling.
📊
What Makes Ours Different
Real-time delivery (under 60 seconds vs. competitors' daily CSVs), AI quality scoring on every lead, weekly automated reports to clients, full fraud detection. We charge the same or less and deliver a significantly better product.
Chapter 02
How a Lead Flows
From the moment a consumer submits a form to the moment the client's phone rings — here is every step the system takes automatically, without any human involvement from us.
1
Consumer Action
Consumer Sees Our Ad and Fills Out the Form
A Google or Meta ad brings the consumer to our landing page. They fill in their name, phone, email, zip code, and describe what they need. They check a consent box and hit submit. Their IP address and the exact time are recorded automatically.
2
Intake Agent
System Validates the Lead
Our automation immediately checks: Is this phone number real? Has this phone or email been submitted before in the last 30 days? Does this look like a bot (submission under 3 seconds)? If it passes all checks, it's saved to our database. If not — it's rejected and logged. The client never sees junk.
3
Qualify Agent
AI Scores the Lead 1–10
Claude AI reads the consumer's message and assigns a quality score. A lead that says "my roof has been leaking for 2 weeks and I need it fixed before winter" scores a 9. A lead that just says "yes" scores a 2. This score goes with the lead so the client knows what they're getting before they call.
4
Route Agent
System Matches Lead to the Right Client
The system checks our Clients database: which client serves roofing in Miami zip codes, has capacity left this month, and is currently active? It assigns the lead to the matching client. If it's an exclusive lead, only one client gets it. If shared, up to 4 clients are notified simultaneously.
5
Deliver Agent
Lead Pushed to Client's CRM + Instant Alert Sent
The lead's contact details are automatically sent to the client's CRM (GoHighLevel, HubSpot, Salesforce, or whatever they use) via a direct connection. Simultaneously, the client gets an SMS: "New lead — [Name], [Phone]. Score 8/10. Call now." And a full email with all details. Total time from form submit to client notification: under 60 seconds.
6
Follow-Up Agent
System Monitors Whether Client Responds
If the client hasn't called the lead within 4 hours, they get an automatic reminder. Another at 24 hours. If they still haven't responded at 48 hours, we re-route the lead to a backup client so it doesn't go to waste — and we flag this client as unresponsive in our system.
7
Report + Billing
Weekly Reports Sent, Monthly Invoices Generated
Every Monday at 8AM, each client automatically receives a report showing their leads, scores, and contact rates. We receive an internal report across all clients. On the 1st of each month, invoices are automatically generated and sent via Stripe. We don't touch any of this manually.
✅

The key point for Sam: Once built, this entire process runs without us. A lead comes in at 3AM on a Sunday — it gets validated, scored, routed, delivered, and the client is alerted before 3:01AM. We build it once, it runs forever.

Chapter 03
The 10 Automated Agents
The system is made up of 10 specialized agents — each one handles a specific job. They run in sequence, pass information between each other, and loop back when something needs to be fixed. Together they replace what would normally require a full operations team.
🔍
1. Intake Agent
Fires: every form submit
  • Checks phone number is real, not VoIP burner
  • Checks email isn't a throwaway domain
  • Detects duplicate submissions
  • Rejects bots based on form timing
  • Saves clean leads to Airtable database
🧠
2. Qualification Agent
Fires: new clean lead detected
  • Sends lead's message to Claude AI
  • AI reads intent and assigns quality score 1–10
  • Tags the niche (roofing, HVAC, legal, etc.)
  • Flags high-value leads for priority routing
  • Saves score and reasoning to database
🗺️
3. Route Agent
Fires: lead is scored
  • Matches lead to client by niche and zip
  • Checks client hasn't hit monthly cap
  • Enforces exclusive vs. shared rules
  • Holds unmatched leads in queue 7 days
  • Alerts us if no client matches
⚡
4. Delivery Agent
Fires: client assigned
  • Pushes lead data to client's CRM via webhook
  • Sends instant SMS alert to client
  • Sends full email with all lead details
  • Retries up to 3 times if CRM is down
  • Logs every delivery attempt with result
⏰
5. Follow-Up Agent
Fires: every 4 hours
  • Finds leads delivered with no client action
  • Sends reminder at 4hrs and 24hrs
  • Re-routes to backup client at 48hrs
  • Tracks each client's response rate
  • Flags unresponsive clients for us
📊
6. Report Agent
Fires: every Monday 8AM
  • Pulls last 7 days of lead data per client
  • Generates client-facing report (their data only)
  • Generates our internal report (all clients)
  • Emails reports automatically — no manual work
  • Includes seasonal trend context per niche
💳
7. Billing Agent
Fires: 1st of each month
  • Counts all delivered leads per client
  • Deducts approved bad-lead credits
  • Creates invoice in Stripe automatically
  • Sends invoice link to client via email
  • Flags unpaid invoices after 7 days
🎛️
8. Management Agent
Fires: when any agent has an issue
  • Watches all other agents for errors
  • Decides what happens when things go wrong
  • Routes failures to retry queues
  • Sends us an SMS when our decision is needed
  • Ensures no lead ever falls through the cracks
🚦
9. Token Police Agent
Fires: every morning at 7AM
  • Tracks daily AI (Claude) API spending
  • Tracks Make.com automation operation usage
  • Tracks Airtable record count
  • Switches to cheaper AI model if near budget
  • Alerts us before we hit any limits
🔴
10. Red Team Agent
Fires: every night at midnight
  • Audits all leads from the past 24 hours
  • Detects IP flooding and bot patterns
  • Cross-checks billing vs. delivery records
  • Flags any scoring anomalies
  • Emails us a nightly health report: ✅ or 🔴

Chapter 04
Risks We've Identified and Solved
We researched what causes lead generation companies to fail, lose clients, get sued, or go out of business. Every vulnerability below has been addressed in how we've designed our system before we built a single line of automation.
Critical Risks — Business-Ending if Ignored
🤖
Lead Farm Fraud — Fake Human Submissions
CRITICAL
Organized operations pay people $0.01 to fill out forms with real-looking but fake data. The person submitting has no interest in the service. The phone number exists but belongs to a stranger. Clients call confused strangers and demand refunds. Industry average: 36% of leads are fraudulent.
Our Solution
Carrier lookup (is this a VoIP burner?), email domain age check, form submission timing analysis. Our Intake Agent rejects these before they ever reach our database.
⚖️
TCPA Violations — Calling Without Legal Consent
CRITICAL
The Telephone Consumer Protection Act requires documented written consent before businesses call or text using an auto-dialer. Most lead gen companies skip this. One class action lawsuit = $500,000+ average settlement. This is the #1 legal risk in the industry.
Our Solution
Every form has a clear, non-pre-checked consent checkbox with specific legal language. The exact consent text, timestamp, and IP are recorded in our database for every single lead. This is our legal proof if we're ever challenged.
♻️
Aged Lead Reselling — Selling Stale Leads as Fresh
CRITICAL
A common industry scam: hold leads for 30–90 days, resell them as new. The consumer has long since solved their problem. Clients call people who have no idea what they're talking about. Destroys conversion rates and trust instantly.
Our Solution
Every lead delivered includes the exact timestamp it was created. Our system only delivers leads under 24 hours old. Leads in the unrouted queue expire after 7 days and are marked expired, not sold.
High Severity Risks
⏱️
Lead Latency — Slow Delivery Kills Conversion Rates
HIGH
Most lead gen companies deliver leads in a daily CSV email. By the time a client calls, the lead is 8–24 hours old. Studies show conversion drops 80% after 5 minutes and 90% after an hour. Clients blame lead quality — but it's a delivery problem.
Our Solution
Our Delivery Agent fires within seconds of a lead being validated. Client receives SMS + email and the lead appears in their CRM in under 60 seconds. This alone makes our leads 3–5x more valuable than competitors.
🎯
Bad Lead Credit Abuse — Clients Gaming the Refund System
HIGH
Clients learn they can claim any lead as "bad" to get credits — even ones they successfully converted. Without tracking what happens to leads after delivery, there's no way to verify. Unchecked, this makes the business unprofitable.
Our Solution
Credits require submission within 72 hours of delivery with a call attempt log. Red Team Agent cross-checks: if a client's CRM shows they interacted with a lead, a "bad lead" claim on that lead is flagged immediately. Monthly credit rate tracked per client.
🔁
Exclusive Lead Double-Selling
HIGH
Client pays a premium for exclusive leads — meaning only they get it. Without technical enforcement, the same lead accidentally (or deliberately) goes to multiple clients. When clients compare notes, trust is destroyed instantly.
Our Solution
Exclusivity is enforced technically, not by policy. Our Route Agent checks the pricing model and uses an atomic database write to prevent two clients from ever being assigned the same exclusive lead. Red Team audits this nightly.
Legal Risks
🔒
GDPR / CCPA — No Data Deletion Policy
LEGAL
Any consumer in California or the EU can legally request their data be deleted. Most lead gen companies have no process. CCPA fines are $100–$750 per consumer per incident. GDPR can fine up to 4% of global revenue.
Our Solution
Leads auto-archive after 90 days. A deletion request email triggers an automated workflow that removes the record from our database and logs the deletion. Privacy policy linked on every form page.
📋
No Signed Client Contracts
LEGAL
Running on verbal agreements means no enforceable lead quality standards, no payment terms, no liability limits. When a client disputes and stops paying, you have nothing to stand on legally.
Our Solution
Every client signs a standard agreement before their first lead is sent. It covers: lead quality definition, credit policy (72hr window, 15% max), payment terms (net 7), exclusivity definition, data handling, and limitation of liability.

Six tools run the entire operation. Every tool has a free tier sufficient to launch and validate the business. Costs only grow once revenue is flowing.
Airtable
Our master lead database. Every lead, every client, every invoice lives here. The command center. Visual, filterable, shareable.
Free — up to 1,000 records
Make.com
The automation engine. Runs all 10 agents as automated scenarios. Connects every other tool together. The brain of the operation.
Free — 1,000 ops/month then $9/mo
Claude API
The AI that scores every lead 1–10 before delivery. Runs inside Make.com. No other lead gen company at our price point does this.
~$5–20/month at scale
Twilio
Sends instant SMS alerts to clients when a lead arrives. Also used for our own internal alerts from the Management Agent.
~$0.007/SMS — essentially free at start

2.9% + $0.30 per transaction only

Where We Start
The most important decision before building anything is picking the right niche and territory to start with. Everything else follows from that choice.
The Right Starting Point
1️⃣
Pick One Niche
Start with a single high-value niche — Roofing is ideal. High average job value ($5,000–$15,000), strong demand year-round in warm climates, high lead prices ($50–$100), and business owners who understand the value of quality leads.
2️⃣
Pick One Territory
One city or metro to start. Miami, Tampa, Houston, Phoenix, Dallas — all strong markets for home services. Easier to dominate one market than be spread thin across many. Proves the model before scaling geographically.
3️⃣
Sign First Client Before First Ad
Reach out to 5–10 roofing companies in your chosen city. Offer a trial batch of 10 leads at a discounted rate. Having a signed buyer before you spend on ads means every lead you generate has a home to go to.
4️⃣
Build the System, Then Run Ads
Complete all 7 build phases first. Test with a fake lead end-to-end. Only turn on paid traffic once every piece of the automation is confirmed working. Spending ad budget before the system is ready = leads that can't be delivered.
