Building the
Lead System
Step by Step

This document walks you and Sam through every tool, every step, and every decision needed to launch onkooz's automated lead generation and distribution business. No technical experience required.

7 phases 28 steps ~2 weeks to launch $0 to start
Phase 1 of 7
Set Up Airtable — Your Lead Database
Airtable is where every lead lives. Think of it as your master spreadsheet that every other tool reads from and writes to. Everything else in this system plugs into Airtable. This is the foundation — get it right first.
1
Create your Airtable account
Sign up for free. No credit card needed. The free plan gives you up to 1,000 records per base — enough to start.
5 min
🌐
Go to airtable.com
Click "Sign up for free" — use your onkooz Google account to keep everything under one login.
➕
Create a new Base
Click "Add a base" → "Start from scratch" → name it onkooz-leads. A base is like a folder that holds all your tables.
💡

Why Airtable? It's free, connects to everything, and gives you a visual dashboard to see all your leads at a glance. Unlike a regular spreadsheet, other tools can automatically add and update records here — no manual work.

2
Build the Leads table — your most important table
Every lead that comes in gets a row here. This table tracks the lead from the moment they fill out the form until they're closed or lost.
20 min
📋
Rename the default table to "Leads"
Double-click "Table 1" at the top and rename it to Leads.
➕
Add these fields one by one
Click the + button at the end of the column headers to add each field below.
Field NameTypeWhat it stores
full_nameSingle line textConsumer's name from the form
emailEmailTheir email address
phonePhone numberTheir phone number
zip_codeSingle line textTheir zip — used to match them to the right client
messageLong textWhat they need (e.g. "my roof is leaking")
sourceSingle selectAdd options: Google, Meta, Instagram, Organic
quality_scoreNumberAI gives this a score 1–10 (filled automatically later)
statusSingle selectAdd: New, Validated, Scored, Routed, Delivered, Contacted, Closed, Lost, Rejected
assigned_clientSingle line textWhich client gets this lead (will become a Link field later)
billing_idSingle line textUnique ID to prevent double-billing (filled automatically)
delivered_atDateWhen the lead was sent to the client
followup_countNumberHow many reminders have been sent to the client
ip_addressSingle line textConsumer's IP — for fraud detection
consent_timestampDateWhen they agreed to be contacted — your legal proof
archivedCheckboxChecked automatically after 90 days
escalatedCheckboxChecked if something needs your attention
escalation_reasonLong textWhat went wrong — written by the Management Agent
💡

Why so many fields? Each field is used by a different agent in the system. The status field is especially important — every agent updates it so you always know exactly where each lead is in the process.

3
Build the Clients table
One row per business buying leads from you. The routing system uses this table to decide which client gets which lead.
15 min
➕
Add a new table called "Clients"
Click the + tab at the bottom of the screen next to your Leads table → name it Clients.
Field NameTypeWhat it stores
business_nameSingle line textName of the business (e.g. "Miami Roofing Co")
contact_nameSingle line textThe person you deal with
emailEmailWhere reports and lead alerts go
phonePhone numberWhere SMS alerts go
nicheSingle selectRoofing, HVAC, Legal, Dental, etc.
service_zipsLong textZip codes they cover, comma separated
crm_typeSingle selectGoHighLevel, HubSpot, Zoho, Salesforce, Other
webhook_urlURLWhere their CRM receives the lead data
monthly_lead_capNumberMax leads they want per month
pricing_modelSingle selectPer-lead, Shared, Exclusive
price_per_leadCurrencyWhat you charge them per lead
statusSingle selectActive, Paused, Unresponsive, Churned
stripe_customer_idSingle line textTheir Stripe ID for auto-billing (added later)
response_rateNumber% of leads they contact within 24hrs (auto-calculated)
joined_dateDateWhen they became a client
⚠️ Add your first client row manually as a test. Use a real business you plan to work with. You'll use this row to test the whole system end to end before going live.
4
Create 4 more tables
You need 6 more tables for delivery tracking, billing, token monitoring, audit logs, and reports. These are simpler to set up.
20 min
📬
Create table: "Delivery Log"
Fields: lead_id (text), client_name (text), attempt_number (number), attempted_at (date), result (select: Success/Failed/Pending), error_message (long text). This records every time we try to send a lead to a client's CRM.
💳
Create table: "Billing"
Fields: client_name (text), period_start (date), period_end (date), leads_delivered (number), credits_applied (number), amount_due (currency), status (select: Draft/Sent/Paid/Overdue), stripe_invoice_id (text).
🚦
Create table: "Token Usage"
Fields: date (date), claude_tokens_used (number), make_ops_used (number), airtable_record_count (number), model_used (select: Sonnet/Haiku/Rule-based), budget_status (select: OK/Warning/Critical). The Token Police Agent writes here daily.
🔴
Create table: "Audit Log"
Fields: audit_date (date), agent (select: Red Team/Token Police/Management), finding_type (select: Bot Flood/Billing Discrepancy/Delivery Failure/Anomaly), severity (select: Low/Medium/High/Critical), description (long text), resolved (checkbox).
Phase 2 of 7
Build the Lead Capture Form in Framer
This is the form consumers fill out on your website. When they hit submit, the data flows directly into Airtable automatically. You already have a Framer site — we're adding a new page to it.
5
Create a new page in Framer for lead capture
This page will have one job: get the consumer's contact information and what they need. Clean, simple, no distractions.
30 min
📄
Open your onkooz Framer project
In the left panel, click the Pages icon → click + to add a new page → name it something like get-quotes or free-estimate.
📝
Add a Form component
In Framer, press Cmd+K → search "Form" → drag a form onto the page. Add fields for: Name, Email, Phone, Zip Code, and a text area for "What do you need?"
⚖️
Add the TCPA consent checkbox — REQUIRED
This is your legal protection. Add a checkbox (not pre-checked) with this text: "By submitting this form I agree to be contacted by onkooz partner businesses by phone, text, and email regarding my request. I agree to the Privacy Policy and Terms."
🔗
Connect the form to Airtable
In Framer's form settings, look for "Submit Action" → select "Send to Webhook" → paste your Airtable webhook URL (you'll get this in Phase 3 from Make.com). For now, you can use a free tool called formspree.io as a temporary bridge.
💡

Keep it simple: The fewer fields on the form, the higher the conversion rate. Name, phone, email, zip, and what they need is all you need. Every extra field you add drops submissions by ~10%.

6
Add a Thank You page
After submitting, consumers need to see a confirmation. This also builds trust and reduces bounce from people who wonder if the form worked.
10 min
✅
Create a /thank-you page
Simple message: "Thanks! A local [niche] specialist will contact you within 24 hours." Add your onkooz logo and a phone number in case they want to call directly. Redirect the form to this page on successful submission.
Phase 3 of 7
Set Up Make.com — The Automation Engine
Make.com is the brain that connects everything. When a form is submitted, Make.com receives it, validates it, scores it, routes it to the right client, and pushes it to their CRM. All automatically, 24/7, without you touching anything.
7
Create your Make.com account
Make.com's free plan gives you 1,000 operations per month. One lead going through the full system uses about 12–15 operations — so you can process roughly 65 leads for free before needing to upgrade to $9/mo.
5 min
🌐
Go to make.com
Sign up free. Use your onkooz email. No credit card needed for the free plan.
🔗
Connect Make.com to Airtable
In Make.com, go to Connections → search "Airtable" → click Connect → it will ask you to log into Airtable and grant access. Select your onkooz-leads base.
8
Build Scenario 1 — Intake Agent
This is the first scenario that runs every time a form is submitted. It validates the lead and saves it to Airtable if it passes. It rejects junk before it ever enters your database.
30 min
➕
Create a new Scenario
Click "Create a new scenario" → click the + circle → search for "Webhooks" → select "Custom webhook". Copy the webhook URL it gives you — this is what you paste into your Framer form's submit action.
🔍
Add a Filter for duplicate detection
After the webhook trigger, add a "Filter" module. Set the condition: search Airtable Leads table for the same phone number submitted in the last 30 days. If found → stop the scenario (don't save the duplicate).
📝
Add "Create Record" in Airtable
Add an Airtable module → "Create a Record" → select your Leads table → map each form field to the matching Airtable field. Set status to New, set consent_timestamp to the current time.
Form Submit → Make.com Webhook → Duplicate Check → Save to Airtable
9
Build Scenario 2 — Qualify + Route Agent
This runs automatically when a new lead appears in Airtable. It sends the lead's message to Claude AI for scoring, then matches the lead to the right client based on niche and zip code.
45 min
👁️
Trigger: Watch Airtable for new leads
Start a new scenario → add Airtable module → "Watch Records" → select Leads table → filter: status = New. Make.com will check every 15 minutes for new leads.
🧠
Add Claude AI scoring via HTTP module
Add an "HTTP" module → "Make a request" → URL: https://api.anthropic.com/v1/messages. In the body, send the lead's message field and ask Claude to score it 1–10. You'll need your Claude API key from console.anthropic.com.
🗺️
Search Clients table for a match
Add Airtable module → "Search Records" → Clients table → filter by niche matching the lead's niche AND service_zips containing the lead's zip. This finds which client should get this lead.
✏️
Update the lead record with score + client
Add Airtable → "Update Record" → on the Leads record, set: quality_score = the AI score, assigned_client = the matching client's name, status = Routed.
10
Build Scenario 3 — Delivery Agent
This fires when a lead is marked "Routed." It sends the lead data to the client's CRM via webhook and alerts the client by SMS and email instantly.
30 min
👁️
Trigger: Watch for status = Routed
New scenario → Airtable "Watch Records" → Leads table → filter: status = Routed.
📤
POST to the client's CRM webhook
HTTP module → POST request → URL = the client's webhook_url from Airtable (pulled from the Clients table lookup). Body: lead's name, phone, email, zip, message, score, and timestamp. This creates a contact in their CRM automatically.
📱
Send client an SMS alert
Add Twilio module → "Send an SMS" → To: client's phone from Airtable → Message: "New lead from onkooz: [Name] in [Zip] needs [Niche]. Call [Phone] now. Score: [Score]/10."
📧
Send client an email with full lead details
Add Resend or Gmail module → To: client's email → Subject: "New Lead — [Name], [Zip]" → Body: all lead fields. Update lead status to Delivered and set delivered_at to now.
⚠️ Retry logic: In Make.com, in the scenario settings, enable "Auto-retry on error" — set to 3 retries with delays. If all 3 fail, the lead status updates to "Delivery Failed" and you get an alert.
Phase 4 of 7
Set Up SMS + Email — Twilio & Resend
Two tools handle all communication. Twilio sends SMS text alerts to clients the moment a lead arrives. Resend sends all emails — lead details, reports, invoices. Both connect directly into Make.com.
11
Set up Twilio for SMS
Twilio gives you a real phone number that Make.com uses to text clients. Costs about $0.007 per SMS — essentially free at your starting volume.
15 min
🌐
Go to twilio.com → sign up free
The free trial gives you $15 credit — enough for ~2,000 SMS messages. Go to Console → Phone Numbers → Get a number. Pick any US number.
🔑
Copy your Account SID and Auth Token
In Twilio Console → Dashboard → copy your Account SID and Auth Token. In Make.com, when you add a Twilio module, paste these in to connect the accounts.
12
Set up Resend for email
Resend is a modern email tool. Free plan sends 3,000 emails/month. You'll use it for lead delivery emails, weekly reports, and invoice notifications.
15 min
🌐
Go to resend.com → sign up free
Connect your domain (e.g. onkooz.com) so emails come from leads@onkooz.com — not a generic address. Follow their DNS setup guide — takes about 10 minutes.
🔑
Copy your Resend API key
In Resend → API Keys → Create API Key → copy it. In Make.com, add an HTTP module to call Resend's API and paste your key in the authorization header.
Phase 5 of 7
Build Follow-Up + Reporting Scenarios
These two scenarios run on a schedule — not triggered by events. The Follow-Up Agent checks every few hours for unresponded leads. The Report Agent runs every Monday morning and emails everyone their report automatically.
13
Build the Follow-Up Agent scenario
This runs every 4 hours. It finds leads delivered more than 4 hours ago with no client response and sends a reminder. Maximum 2 reminders — then it re-routes the lead.
25 min
⏰
Trigger: Schedule — every 4 hours
New scenario → click the trigger → "Scheduling" → set to run every 4 hours.
🔍
Search for leads needing follow-up
Airtable → "Search Records" → Leads table → filter: status = Delivered AND delivered_at is more than 4 hours ago AND followup_count is less than 2.
📱
Send reminder to client + update count
Twilio SMS to client: "Reminder: You have an uncalled lead from [Name] — [Phone]. Please contact them ASAP." Then update the lead's followup_count + 1 in Airtable.
14
Build the Report Agent scenario
Runs every Monday at 8AM automatically. Pulls last week's data, builds two reports — one for you (all clients), one for each client (their leads only) — and emails them out. Zero manual work.
40 min
📅
Trigger: Schedule — every Monday at 8AM
New scenario → Scheduling trigger → set day: Monday, time: 8:00 AM.
📊
Pull last 7 days of leads per client
Airtable → "Search Records" → Leads table → filter: created_at in last 7 days. Group by assigned_client. For each client, count total leads, delivered, contacted, closed, and average quality score.
📧
Email each client their report
Loop through each client → Resend email to their address. Subject: "Your onkooz Lead Report — Week of [Date]". Body includes: leads received, average score, how many were contacted, and a link to their Airtable shared view for full details.
📧
Email yourself the internal report
Send a second email to your onkooz address with ALL clients in one report: total leads generated, total revenue, any clients with high credit rates, any delivery failures from the week, and Make.com op usage vs. limit.
Phase 6 of 7
Set Up Stripe Billing
Stripe handles all client payments. The Billing Agent counts how many leads each client received, generates an invoice, and either auto-charges their card or emails them an invoice link — automatically, at the end of each billing cycle.
15
Set up your Stripe account
Stripe is free to use — they take 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction. No monthly fee. You only pay when you collect money.
20 min
🌐
Go to stripe.com → create account
Sign up with your onkooz email. Complete business verification (takes 1–2 days to fully activate). Add your bank account for payouts.
👤
Create a Customer in Stripe for each client
In Stripe → Customers → + New → add each client's business name, email, and card on file. Copy their Customer ID (starts with cus_) and paste it into the stripe_customer_id field in your Airtable Clients table.
🔑
Connect Stripe to Make.com
In Make.com → Connections → search "Stripe" → paste your Stripe Secret Key (from Stripe Dashboard → Developers → API Keys). Now Make.com can create and send invoices automatically.
16
Build the Billing Agent scenario
Runs on the 1st of every month. Counts each client's delivered leads, subtracts any approved credits, calculates the amount owed, and sends the invoice through Stripe.
30 min
📅
Trigger: 1st of every month at 9AM
Scheduling trigger → Monthly → Day 1 → 9:00 AM.
🔢
Count delivered leads per client
Airtable → Search leads where status = Delivered AND assigned_client = [client] AND delivered_at is in the previous month. Count them. Check Billing table for any approved credits for this client this month. Subtract credits from total.
💳
Create and send invoice via Stripe
Stripe module → "Create Invoice Item" → customer = client's stripe_customer_id → amount = net_leads × price_per_lead. Then "Finalize and Send Invoice." Stripe emails the invoice link to the client automatically.
📝
Log invoice in Airtable Billing table
Create a new record in the Billing table with all the invoice details and the Stripe invoice ID. This gives you a permanent record and lets the Red Team Agent verify billing accuracy.
Phase 7 of 7
Add the Management, Token Police + Red Team Agents
These three agents are your safety net. They don't generate leads or send invoices — they watch everything else and catch problems before they cost you money or clients.
17
Build the Token Police Agent
Runs every morning. Checks how much you've spent on Claude API and Make.com operations. Alerts you before you hit limits. Switches to cheaper AI mode if needed.
20 min
📅
Trigger: Every day at 7AM
Runs before the business day starts so you know your budget status each morning.
🔢
Pull yesterday's token usage from Airtable
Search Token Usage table for today's date. Compare claude_cost_usd against your $2/day limit and make_ops_used against your monthly limit.
📱
Alert you if approaching limits
If daily AI cost > $1.50 → SMS yourself: "⚠️ AI budget at 75% today — switching to Haiku model." If Make.com ops > 700 this month → SMS: "⚠️ 700/1000 Make.com ops used — consider upgrading."
18
Build the Red Team Agent
Runs every night at midnight. Audits the past 24 hours of activity looking for fraud, billing discrepancies, and delivery failures. Sends you a green or red status report each morning.
30 min
🕛
Trigger: Every night at midnight
Scheduling → Daily → 12:00 AM.
🔍
Check 1: IP flood detection
Search Leads where created_at = today. Group by ip_address. If any IP has more than 3 leads today → flag those leads as quarantined, write to Audit Log, alert yourself.
🔍
Check 2: Delivery vs. billing cross-check
Count leads with status = Delivered this month per client. Compare to what's in the Billing table for this month. If numbers don't match → write to Audit Log with severity = High.
📧
Email yourself the nightly audit report
If no issues found → email: "✅ Audit clean — 0 anomalies detected." If issues found → email a list of each finding with severity. You wake up knowing your system's health.
