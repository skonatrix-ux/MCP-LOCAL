Phase 2 — Lead Captured (Your Database)
Airtable — Free 🗄️
Your Lead Database
Every lead lands here first. You see: all contact info, which niche, which client it's assigned to, timestamp, and current status. This is your master record — always.
triggers automation
Phase 3 — Automated Distribution
Make.com — Free Tier ⚡
Automation Engine
The moment a lead hits Airtable, Make.com fires. It:

1. Identifies which client gets this lead
2. Pushes contact data to their CRM via webhook
3. Sends the client an instant email/SMS alert
4. Updates lead status in Airtable to "Delivered"
pushes to client
Phase 4 — Delivered to Client CRM
If client uses GHL 🏢
GHL Sub-Account
Lead appears directly in their pipeline. You can see their account as the agency admin — full visibility into contact status, notes, pipeline stage.
If client uses other CRM 🏢
HubSpot / Zoho / Any CRM
Make.com posts lead data via webhook or API to whatever CRM they use. Lead appears in their system automatically. Status updates flow back optionally.
status updates back
Phase 5 — Your Visibility
onkooz Dashboard 👁️
You See Everything
Back in your Airtable dashboard you track every lead across all clients:

· Delivered — sent to client CRM
· Contacted — client reached out
· Closed — deal won
· Bad Lead — refund/replace

You can filter by client, niche, date, or status at any time.

You never lose the lead. Even after it's sent to a client's CRM, your Airtable master record stays intact. The lead belongs to onkooz first — always.
